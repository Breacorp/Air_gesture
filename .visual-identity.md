# Visual Identity Guidelines: Moderno Tech

This document serves as the single source of truth for the visual identity of all Air Gesture products. ANY deviation from these guidelines is considered a bug.

## 🚫 ABSOLUTELY FORBIDDEN (Anti-Patterns)
- NO Cyberpunk/Neon aesthetics.
- NO excessive glow, `shadowBlur` on everything, or "electric" neon colors (#00f5d4, #ff007f).
- NO "retro-gaming" or "80s arcade" styles.
- NO stark, high-contrast flickering effects.

## ✅ MANDATORY STYLE: "Moderno Elegance"
The visual identity must align perfectly with [moderno.com.ar](https://www.moderno.com.ar/).

### 1. Color Palette
- Primary Background: Deep, sophisticated darks (near-black, charcoal, midnight blue).
- Accents: White, silver, and subtle, professional blues.
- Surfaces: Semi-transparent "Glassmorphism" (white/grey with low opacity + backdrop-blur).

### 2. Geometry & UI
- Minimalism: Maximize white space and clean lines.
- Softness: Large border-radius (rounded corners) for all cards and buttons.
- Depth: Subtle, soft shadows instead of glows. Use depth to indicate hierarchy.

### 3. Motion & Interaction
- Fluidity: Organic transitions (ease-in-out).
- Subtlety: Micro-interactions that feel premium and intentional.
- Precision: Clean, thin lines for telemetry and indicators.

### 4. Brand Alignment
- Logo: Use the official Moderno Tech logo.
- Typography: Clean, modern sans-serif fonts (Space Grotesk, Inter, or similar).
- Language: Professional, minimal, and high-end.

## Implementation Rule
Before adding any visual effect, ask: "Does this look like it belongs on moderno.com.ar?". If the answer is "No" or "It looks like a neon sign", DELETE IT.
